The company introduced a remote work policy in early 2024. Prior to this change, all employees were required to work from the office five days a week.

Following the introduction of remote work, employee productivity increased by 18% compared to the previous year. The HR department attributed this improvement to reduced commute times and improved work-life balance.

However, several employees reported difficulty communicating with their teams. Coordination across departments became more challenging, and response times to internal requests increased.

The company therefore introduced mandatory weekly team meetings to improve communication and collaboration. These meetings were conducted via video conferencing every Monday morning.

The new meeting structure helped reduce communication problems. By the end of 2024, employee satisfaction scores rose by 12 points.

Nevertheless, some employees reported that the increase in meetings negatively affected their productivity. A small survey conducted in December 2024 found that 23% of employees felt that the meeting frequency was too high.

The company plans to review its remote work policy in early 2025. Senior management has stated that they expect remote work to remain a core part of the company strategy.
